## Hello, I’m Adolfo Hilario-Garcia

I am a physics graduate based in Munich and affiliated with the Max Planck Institute for Physics.

I completed a B.Sc. in Physics at the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona and an M.Sc. in Theoretical and Mathematical Physics at Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich and the Technical University of Munich.

## Background

My academic background is in physics, with graduate training focused on theoretical and mathematical approaches. This website is a home for my academic profile and future research and project updates.
